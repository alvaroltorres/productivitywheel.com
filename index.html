Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ProductivityWheel.

ProductivityWheel tracks mouse movement velocity, direction variance, click rates, scroll activity, and keyboard strokes per minute. It never records which keys you press, which apps you use, or captures any screenshots. All tracking is local — nothing is sent anywhere.

Yes. ProductivityWheel uses a Zero-Network architecture. The app makes zero internet connections during normal use. All data is stored locally on your machine in an encrypted SQLite database. The only network call ever made is a one-time license activation (PRO users only).

The Focus Score (0–100) uses an exponential-smoothing algorithm that weighs mouse velocity (25%), keyboard rate (30%), clicks (15%), scroll (10%), and consistency (20%). It starts at 50, ramps fast during focused work, and decays slowly during idle periods.

When paused (via tray or button), or when your PC sleeps or locks, the Focus Score and wheel freeze completely — no decay, no change. On resume, it continues from exactly where you left off with no idle penalty.

The Free version includes the full spinning wheel overlay, live Focus Score, and auto-pause. PRO unlocks work modes (Hardcore, Ultra Hardcore, Reading, Creative), smart audio/visual alerts, session history, and advanced customization. PRO is a one-time purchase — yours forever.
